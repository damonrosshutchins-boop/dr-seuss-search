The pinned distribution is copied from npm by the .github/workflows/vendor-ultraviolet.yml workflow and checked into this public repo. The local uv.config.js and sw.js are customized for the site's GitHub Pages subpath and isolated ultraviolet service-worker scope.

Ultraviolet is optional/experimental. The parent interface uses a sandboxed iframe without allow-same-origin to protect its Supabase sessions. This isolation can limit compatibility with some proxied sites, and the site's normal safety reporting cannot observe every internal navigation.
